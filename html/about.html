About Me

Product Designer & Visual Storyteller

Hi, I'm Franca!

Combining empathy, curiosity, and medieval-gothic aesthetics to craft designs that click on a human level.

As a product designer, I’ve always been someone who loves to dive deep into a concept, figure out what makes it tick, and translate that feeling into a design that truly fits the user. For me, it’s all about having a sharp eye for atmosphere and detail, making sure a concept doesn't just look good, but actually clicks on a human level.

My path to design has been a bit unconventional. Before focusing fully on product design, I studied Safety and Security Management. That background taught me how to genuinely help people, make complex information clear, and design with real societal impact in mind. On top of that, I’ve spent years creating content and building an online audience, including an official partnership with Lionsgate, which taught me how to tell stories that capture attention and connect with people on social media platforms such as Instagram.

Outside of standard design work, I love pouring my creativity into personal projects. I’m endlessly inspired by medieval and gothic aesthetics, which you can see in my experimental custom typography, tactile layouts, and moody, dark-luxury concepts. I believe the best work comes from a mix of curiosity, empathy, and just letting yourself get a little lost in the creative process.
